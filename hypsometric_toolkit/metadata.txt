[general]
name=Hypsometric Analysis Toolkit
qgisMinimumVersion=3.16
description=Automates the Raster Terrain Analysis "Hypsometric Curves" algorithm and computes hypsometric integrals with morphological interpretation.
about=Runs qgis:hypsometriccurves on a DEM + boundary polygon layer, caches the per-feature CSV outputs, computes the hypsometric integral (HI) for each feature (trapezoidal curve integration and elevation-relief ratio), classifies the landform stage (Youthful / Mature / Old), plots the curves, and writes a summary CSV compatible with the standalone hypsometric_analysis_v2.py script.
version=1.7.2
changelog=1.7.2 - Fix: small craters were measured badly. Rays are now capped by the seeding polygon and the DEM is read around it at a resolution scaled to the crater, not the raster
    1.7.1 - Fix: a boundary polygon drawn around a crater truncated every rim ray at its edge, producing a badly wrong diameter and depth. The polygon now seeds the search; the rays run on the whole raster
    1.7.0 - CRS sanity checks on the DEM, and an automatic warning when the elevation step is too coarse for a feature's relief
    1.6.1 - Results tab shows d/D; the interpretation moves out of the table but stays in the summary CSV
    1.6.0 - Crater depth, diameter and d/D reported in the summary CSV beside the hypsometric integral
    1.5.0 - Traced rim outline: connect the detected rim picks instead of forcing a circle, for craters that are not round
    1.4.1 - Rim detection: downsample default now scales to the raster, and an over-coarse value explains itself instead of finding nothing
    1.4.0 - Detect rim button: fit a crater rim circle from the DEM, over the whole raster or seeded from polygons, with centre-shift and circle-fit-rms quality attributes
    1.3.0 - Draw polygon button: digitize an area of interest straight onto the map canvas and analyze it, without preparing a boundary layer first
    1.2.0 - Clean button: clears the results, plot and log, and deletes the cached runs after confirmation
    1.1.0 - Strahler-convention plot (a/A on X, h/H on Y) matching hypsometric_analysis_v2.py; pure-Qt plot renderer fallback when matplotlib is unavailable
    1.0.0 - Initial release
